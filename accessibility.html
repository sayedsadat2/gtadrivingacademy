Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Our Commitment

GTA Driving Academy is committed to providing an accessible experience for all students and website visitors, in line with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and its associated standards.

Lessons and In-Person Service

We welcome students of all abilities and will work with you to accommodate specific needs where possible — including adjusted lesson pacing, additional practice time, and communication in the format that works best for you. Let us know your needs when booking so we can prepare accordingly.

Website Accessibility

We aim to make this website usable for the widest possible audience, including people using screen readers or other assistive technology. Ongoing efforts include:

  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and forms
  • Sufficient colour contrast for readability
  • Clear heading structure for screen reader navigation

We recognize this is an ongoing process, and we're committed to continuing to improve accessibility across the site.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website or in our services, please let us know — we want to fix it. Contact us at gtadrivingacademy@gmail.com or +1 (416) 939-0201, and we'll respond as quickly as we can.
