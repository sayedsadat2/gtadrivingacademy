Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

GTA Driving Academy ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's protected, in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Information We Collect

When you book a lesson, contact us, or use this website, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Driver's licence status (G1/G2) and lesson preferences
  • Messages you send through our contact form or booking system
  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, general location, device type)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Schedule and confirm driving lessons
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send booking confirmations and appointment reminders
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and administrative precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party tools (such as booking or contact form processors) that receive limited information necessary to perform their function. These providers are expected to handle your information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information held by us. To make such a request, contact us at gtadrivingacademy@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
